Eating out in Glasgow

Where we ate, what it cost and what we'd order again, plus the Scottish dishes worth trying at least once.

See where we ateGroceries and shopping

Menu prices in Glasgow look like Singapore's, but they are in pounds.

At about S$1.70 to the pound, a £15 main costs more than S$25. Most of us cooked on weekdays and ate out at weekends, with meal deals and Too Good To Go bags for busy days. Groceries and meal deals are on the Shopping tab.

We visited every restaurant in the first lists. The Asian restaurants, student discounts and rules further down come from our own research in October 2026. Restaurants open and close quickly, so check before you go.

Scottish food to try once

  • Haggis, neeps and tatties

    Sheep's offal, oats and spices, with mashed turnip and potato. Try it at least once; some of us loved it.

  • Fish supper

    At a chippy, "supper" means it comes with chips. Sauces and sides cost extra, so a full meal can pass £20.

  • Sticky toffee pudding

    Date sponge soaked in toffee sauce. Our favourite British dessert.

  • Sunday roast

    Roast meat, potatoes, vegetables, gravy and a Yorkshire pudding. Have it at a carvery or a good steakhouse.

  • Cullen skink

    A thick soup of smoked haddock, potato and onion.

  • Pies

    Steak and ale pie, shepherd's pie, and the Scotch pie: a small round mutton pie sold at bakeries.

  • Irn-Bru

    Scotland's bright orange soft drink. Every shop sells it.

  • Deep-fried Mars bar

    If you dare. Opinions in our group were divided.

Tips, water and drinks

Tipping
Not expected at counters, cafés or pubs. For table service, about 10% is usual if the bill has no service charge. Some restaurants add a discretionary service charge of 10–12.5%; you can ask for it to be removed.
Tap water
Free. Ask for "tap water" or you may be sold a bottle.
Buying alcohol
Shops in Scotland can only sell alcohol between 10am and 10pm, and there is a minimum price of 65p per unit. Shops and bars ask anyone who looks under 25 for ID, so carry your passport.
