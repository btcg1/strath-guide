Shopping in Glasgow

Which supermarkets are close to campus, which loyalty apps are worth having, and where to buy everything else you need for a semester in halls.

Start with groceriesEating out

In the photo: Glasgow city centre

Everything you need for a semester in halls is within walking distance of campus.

Aldi on High Street is a short walk away, and the St Enoch Centre and Argyle Street have the rest. Get the loyalty apps on your first day: the lower price on many shelf labels is for members only.

Shops in Scotland have no Sunday trading limits, so big stores open on Sundays too. Prices below were checked in October 2026.

Supermarkets near campus

CheapestAldiHigh St, a short walk from campus. The cheapest for basics. Aldi has no loyalty card.
Better rangeLidlDuke St, a longer walk east than Aldi, but better products. Get the Lidl Plus app for weekly coupons.
EverywhereTesco ExpressSeveral in the centre. Prices with a Clubcard are often lower than the shelf price.
EverywhereSainsbury's LocalSeveral in the centre. Nectar Prices are lower prices on selected items for Nectar members.
EverywhereCo-opMembership costs £1, and you get the £1 back on your first shop.

Cheapest of allPoundlandBasics at the lowest prices.

Asian groceries

Chinese supermarketChung Ying254 Dobbie's Loan, near Glasgow Caledonian University. Pricier than the supermarkets, but it has the sauces, noodles, rice and snacks you will miss. For us it was worth it.

Your first week in halls

Kitchen and beddingTJ HughesGround floor of the St Enoch Centre. Cookware, bed sheets, blankets and laundry baskets. Share an Uber back to halls; split four ways it is cheap.
Towels and basicsPrimark56–68 Argyle St. Cheap towels, bedding, clothes and shoes.
FreeLeftovers from the last residentsHalls usually have a pile or a market of things previous students left behind. Check it before you buy anything.

Clothes and gear

ThermalsUniqloSt Enoch Centre, open since October 2025. An Extra Warm Heattech inner layer got us through the winter.
Cheap layersPrimarkArgyle St. Thermals, gloves, hats and scarves.
Hiking gearMountain WarehouseWaterproof jackets and walking boots for the Highlands.
Second-handThe BarrasWeekend market in the East End, Saturdays and Sundays from 10am to 4pm.

Saving money

Too Good To Go
Cafés, bakeries and supermarkets sell their unsold food at closing time in surprise bags, at half price or less. This saved us a lot of money.
Olio
A free app where neighbours and shops give away food and household items.
Bags
Every shop in Scotland charges at least 10p for a carrier bag, so keep a tote bag with you.
Pharmacy
Boots and Superdrug both have several branches in the centre. Pharmacists can advise on minor illnesses without an appointment.
