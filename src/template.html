Questions we had

Are lectures recorded?

Each lecturer decides, and none of ours were. Attendance is taken differently in every class (QR code, password or paper), so go to everything in week 1. MAE and EEE turned out to be relaxed.

When do exam timetables come out?

About five weeks before exams, on Pegasus: the first week of November for semester 1 and early March for semester 2. Each is a draft for one week, then final. In 2026/27 the assessment periods are 7–18 December and 19 April – 21 May.

Which hall should I pick?

Thomas Campbell Court: 4-bedroom flats, 1 shared bathroom, £146.16 a week. Birkbeck Court: 6-bedroom flats, 2 bathrooms, £146.16. Andrew Ure, Forbes and Garnett: 4- or 6-bedroom flats, rooms with a wash basin, £154.56. Single-gender flats felt roomier.

What does a semester in halls cost?

Semester 1 let (14 weeks 2 days): about £2,090 for a standard room, £2,210 with a basin. Semester 2 let (21 weeks 5 days): about £3,170 or £3,360. Semester 1 rooms are limited, so apply early through the Accommodation Portal.

Is the NHS free for me?

Don't count on it. NHS Scotland's free care for students is tied to a student visa, and you enter as a visitor. NTU's Group Travel Insurance (Chubb) covers sanctioned exchange travel; read what it includes.

Which SIM card?

Lebara: 100 GB for £20 per 30 days, EU roaming included. VOXI: eSIM, but Europe needs a roaming pass (£2.60 a day, £15 for 8 days, £20 for 15 days).

How much cash do I need?

About £100 for the whole semester. Cards work almost everywhere, including buses. Open a Monzo account if Groupon or a Direct Debit rejects your Singapore card.

Where do I buy groceries?

ALDI (High St) and LIDL (Duke St) are closest; LIDL has better stock. Savers for cheap brand toiletries. Chung Ying (254 Dobbie's Loan) for Asian food. TJ Hughes (St Enoch Centre) for bedding and pans. Bring two shopping bags: every shop charges for carrier bags.

Which meal deal?

Tesco is £3.85 with Clubcard. Boots is often cheapest and has a clearance shelf. At Co-op take the Ginsters pasty as the snack. Greggs gives a free sausage roll with a cold Sandwich Deal if you show Student Beans.

Is National Trust for Scotland worth it?

£6.60 a month, or £3.80 on the Young rate, but you can't cancel in the first year, so it's really £79.20 (£45.60). Worth it for three or four properties. Under 22 with a National Entitlement Card? Historic Environment Scotland castles cost £1.

What should I eat that's British?

Cullen skink, steak and ale pie, haggis neeps and tatties, sticky toffee pudding. Sunday roast only at a carvery or steakhouse. A chippy meal can pass £20 once you add sauces and sides.

Best hikes?

Old Man of Storr (Skye) was the most scenic, if the wind lets you up. Goatfell on Arran if you take the first ferry over. Arthur's Seat in Edinburgh is easy until the rocky top. We didn't get to Ben Nevis.

Where can I see the northern lights?

Not in Glasgow; it's too bright. Shetland is the best bet in Scotland: NorthLink's overnight ferry from Aberdeen, from £29.50 one way in low season (2027 fares), cabins extra.

What do people do in the evenings?

The Stand's Sunday improv is £1 for students. The Glee Club has student prices on Fridays and Saturdays. Kitty O'Shea's for live music, Maggie's for line dancing, Union nights like Drag Bingo, Strath Active sports.

Anything weird about halls?

The fire alarm is tested every Wednesday, sometimes at 8 am. Laundry at Lord Todd is about S$5 a wash and dry; bring detergent. Kitchens have an oven but no air fryer.
