Shopping in Glasgow

Which supermarkets are close to campus, which loyalty apps are worth having, and where to buy everything else you need for a semester in halls.

Start with groceriesEating out

Everything you need for a semester in halls is within walking distance of campus.

Aldi on High Street is a short walk away, and the St Enoch Centre and Argyle Street have the rest. Get the loyalty apps on your first day: the lower price on many shelf labels is for members only.

Shops in Scotland have no Sunday trading limits, so big stores open on Sundays too. Prices below were checked in October 2026.

Saving money

Too Good To Go
Cafés, bakeries and supermarkets sell their unsold food at closing time in surprise bags, at half price or less. We used it to keep food costs down.
Olio
A free app where neighbours and shops give away food and household items.
Bags
Every shop in Scotland charges at least 10p for a carrier bag, so keep a tote bag with you.
Pharmacy
Boots and Superdrug both have several branches in the centre. Pharmacists can advise on minor illnesses without an appointment.
